Open Source Enterprise Architecture

OpenEA Community is designed for organizations and architecture practitioners looking for an open, self-hosted approach to enterprise architecture management. The repository is the authoritative architecture model, while visualizations, analytics, findings, impact analysis, portfolios, and roadmaps are derived from that structured information.
